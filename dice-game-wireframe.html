How to play

The board

5×5 grid, columns A–E, rows 1–5. The White Ram starts on C1, the Black Ram on C5.

Board: Default. Shaded squares are The Plateau; white squares are The Cliffs. Pick a board in Settings.

Setup

Each player rolls one die. Each roll becomes that ram's starting score (S1–S6). In round 1, lower roll goes first and ties re-roll. In later rounds, the player who landed the last successful hit goes first.

Your turn

Roll two dice. Pick one to move with (R1–R6). The other goes on your ram as its new score (S1–S6). A big number can power your attack or your defense, but not both.

Movement

Moves go in a straight line. "1–2" means you choose 1 or 2 spaces.

R11 space horizontally or vertically
R21 space horizontally, vertically, or diagonally
R31–2 spaces diagonally
R41–2 spaces horizontally or vertically
R51–2 spaces horizontally or vertically, or 1 space diagonally
R61–2 spaces horizontally, vertically, or diagonally

Successful hits

  • Charge onto the other ram's square to land a successful hit. Your score die (your ram's new score) is compared to their ram's score.
  • The Cliffs: lower numbers are stronger. Your score must be equal to or lower than theirs. (S3 hits S3–S6.)
  • The Plateau: higher numbers are stronger. Your score must be equal to or higher than theirs. (S3 hits S1–S3.)
  • The zone of the ram being hit decides which rule applies.

S6 bonus

End your turn as an S6 on the Plateau and you take another turn. Keep going until you end a turn as an S6 on the Cliffs or with any other score on the Plateau.

No legal move

If neither die gives you a legal move, you pass and your ram keeps its current score.

Winning

Each successful hit scores 1 point, and both rams reset to C1 and C5 with a fresh opening roll. The ram with the most successful hits wins the match: best of 3, 5, or 7, or race to 10 (set in Settings).
